> My God, I am sorry for my sins with all my heart. In choosing to do wrong and failing to do good, I have sinned against you whom I should love above all things. 
>
>I firmly intend, with your help, to do penance, to sin no more, and to avoid whatever leads me to sin.
>
>Our Savior Jesus Christ suffered and died for us. In his name, my God, have mercy.

this is a prayer that is said to God to ask for forgiveness for sins. It is a way to express remorse and seek reconciliation with God. The prayer acknowledges the wrongdoing, expresses a desire to change, and asks for divine assistance in avoiding future sins. It also references the sacrifice of Jesus Christ as a source of hope and redemption.
