## My Journey

I was born in the US to a Chinese family and raised in Hangzhou, China. In 2023, I moved back to the United States, giving me a rich background in two distinct cultures. My family heritage spans both Northern and Eastern China.

**I have a deep love for mathematics.** From a young age, I dove into algebra and advanced topics, often studying independently or alongside my mom and my math teachers. I frequently participate in math competitions to challenge myself and deepen my problem-solving skills.

**I also play and compose music.** I have a strong musical sense and love feeling the emotion behind every piece. I regularly participate in piano competitions; some recent achievements include:

- MSMTA Piano Solo Spring Competition (May 2, 2026) - Group 14F: **1st Place**
- Hartman Piano Awards (May 10, 2026) - Group 14: **3rd Place**
- Gottlieb Competition (May 17, 2026) - Group L: **2nd Place**

Beyond performing, I compose original music and publish my sheet music online as I work on new compositions.

## Technology

My coding journey started with Python, where I created my very first application: a math game.

By 7th grade, I discovered web development and brought that math game to the web. I started by building simple static sites—launching my first website in May 2025—and gradually moved toward modern web frameworks like Astro (which powers this site). 

Today, I design complex applications and server solutions, continuing to build technology for the future.

## Faith

Growing up, I was a complete atheist and held various stereotypes about Christianity. However, around Easter 2024, someone gave me a book about Jesus. I initially resisted what I was reading, but as I continued, I found myself drawn to its truth.

After exploring Christianity more deeply, I chose to accept Jesus into my life. I am currently completing my OCIA journey.
